What we check in a licence transfer

Rules differ widely. Some regimes treat a licence as non-transferable and handle changes of ownership through regulator approval, while others allow assignment or sale of the licensed entity. We map the process in your jurisdiction before any money moves.

  • Is the licence active and in good standing, with no open violations?
  • Which conditions attach to the licence, site or people named on it?
  • Who is allowed to own, operate or finance a licensed business there?
  • What approvals, notices and waiting periods does the regulator require?
  • What happens to permits, inventory, contracts and employees?
  • Are taxes, debts and local-authority approvals in order?

Common questions

Can any licence be bought or transferred?

No. It depends on the country, the licence type and the regulator. In some places licences cannot be transferred at all, and in others only certain people or companies may hold them. We check this first so you do not pay for something you cannot lawfully take over.

Do you guarantee that a licence will be approved?

No one can. Regulators decide. We improve your chances by choosing the right route, preparing complete applications and avoiding common errors.

Can you help foreign investors?

Often yes, but some markets limit foreign ownership or restrict certain licences to local nationals. We explain the options and the legal structures that are actually permitted.
