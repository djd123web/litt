Training programs

Cultivation and extraction training

Practical, hands-on programs that take your team from first principles to consistent, compliant production.

Our programs

Each program is tailored to your site, licence type and team. Start with one, or combine them into a full seed-to-oil curriculum.

  • Cultivation

    From site setup to harvest, for indoor, greenhouse and outdoor operations.

    • Facility and environment planning
    • Propagation, nutrition and irrigation
    • Integrated pest and disease management
    • Harvest, drying, curing and storage
    • Record keeping and traceability

  • Strains and genetics

    How to choose and manage varieties for your climate, market and licence.

    • Chemotypes: cannabinoid and terpene profiles
    • Matching strains to medical, cosmetic or industrial goals
    • Adapting varieties to altitude, heat and humidity
    • Selection, stability and crop uniformity
    • Sourcing genetics through certified, regulated channels
    See our strain work in Morocco

  • Oil extraction and processing

    Producing cannabis oil safely, consistently and to a documented standard.

    • Overview of extraction methods and how to choose one
    • Facility layout, equipment safety and hazard controls
    • Post-extraction refinement and formulation
    • Lab testing, stability and batch records
    • Preparing for inspection and audits

  • Quality, compliance and operations

    The systems that keep a licence safe and a product trusted.

    • Standard operating procedures (SOPs)
    • Good agricultural and manufacturing practice
    • Security, inventory and seed-to-sale tracking
    • Staff training, hygiene and safety culture
    • Dealing with regulators and inspectors

Who it is for

  • Licensed growers, cooperatives and processors who want to raise quality and yield consistency
  • Farmers and companies moving from informal to legal production
  • New licensees and investors who need to understand the operation before they commit
  • Pharmaceutical, cosmetic and wellness businesses sourcing from regulated supply chains
  • Managers and technical staff who need clear procedures their teams can follow

Formats

  • On-site cohorts: we come to your farm or facility and teach on your real setup
  • Private team training: a program built around your licence, crop and goals
  • Train-the-trainer: equip your senior staff to teach everyone else
  • Remote theory and follow-up: online sessions and reviews between visits

Length and cost depend on scope and location. Contact us with your goals for a tailored proposal.

What you take away

  • Written procedures

    SOPs and checklists adapted to your facility so the knowledge stays after we leave.

  • Trained people

    Staff who understand the why behind each step, not just the steps.

  • Inspection readiness

    Records, workflows and habits that stand up to regulators and buyers.

Responsible training policy

Lawful only. We deliver hands-on cultivation and extraction training only where those activities are lawful and licensed, and we ask about your licence status before we scope a program. For people who do not yet hold a licence, we teach planning, theory and compliance only.

Our training does not include supplying plants, seeds or cannabis products. Where genetics come up, we focus on how to select varieties and how to source them through regulated, certified channels.

Common questions

Do you only train people who already hold a licence?

Licensed operators get full hands-on programs. Applicants and investors can take planning, theory and compliance modules while they work towards a licence, and we can pair training with our licensing consulting.

Can the program be adapted to my crop and climate?

Yes. Every program starts with a review of your site, climate, licence type and goals, then we build the curriculum around them.

Where do you teach?

On site with clients in Morocco, and we are building programs for Latin America. Tell us where you are and whether it is lawful to operate there, and we will confirm what is possible.

Do you offer training in languages other than English?

Let us know your team's language needs when you contact us and we will confirm what we can support.
