Careers

Interested in working with Litt California? Here is how to reach us, and how to protect yourself from recruitment scams.

Work with us

We are a small team that teaches cultivation and extraction, advises on licensing, and delivers to members in Los Angeles. If you would like to be considered for a role, send a short introduction and your resume to careers@littcalifornia.com.

Important notice: recruitment scam

Beware of fraudulent job offers.

In March 2021, we learned of a spear-phishing scam affecting individuals who may be applying for jobs with our company. Spear phishing is a fraudulent practice of sending emails that appear to come from a known or trusted sender, in order to induce targeted individuals to reveal confidential information.

In this case, the scammer finds individuals who have posted resumes or made applications on popular job sites, then emails them with invitations to interview and even job offers from our company. In connection with the job offers, the scammer seeks personal information, such as completed tax forms or Social Security numbers (SSN). The scammer's offer letter looks real because it uses our logo.

Most of the scam emails appear to come from the following email address: littcalifornia@gmail.com. This is not a valid company email.

Please contact careers@littcalifornia.com directly if you have any questions about the authenticity of communications appearing to come from our company.

How to protect yourself

  • Be cautious about any offer that asks for tax forms, your SSN or other personal information before you have verified who you are dealing with.
  • Do not reply to the suspicious message, and do not open its links or attachments.
  • Write to us at the careers address above to confirm whether a message is genuine.
