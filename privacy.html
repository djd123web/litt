Privacy notice

What we collect through this website, why, and how it is handled.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, email, phone number, delivery area and message, and for membership requests your chosen payment method and a payment reference code. We never collect your bank login, bank account number or card number on this site. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

How we use it

We use it to reply to your enquiry and to decide whether and how we can help. We do not sell your information.

Who processes it

Forms are sent through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, which passes your message to our email inbox. Bank (ACH) payments are authorized on our payment partner's own secure page, and their privacy policy applies there. Zelle payments happen in your bank's app. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Your browser

The delivery and membership pages remember that you confirmed your age for the current browser session only. It is cleared when you close the tab.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your enquiry or correct your details by sending a new message through the contact form.
