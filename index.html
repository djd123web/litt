How we work

Cannabis is regulated differently in every country and often every region. Our approach keeps clients on the right side of those rules while building real skills.

  • Lawful work only. We train and advise people operating within the rules of their jurisdiction.
  • Hands-on and practical. Teaching built around real facilities, real crops and real workflows.
  • Documented. You leave with checklists and procedures your team can keep using.
  • Local counsel involved. We work alongside qualified lawyers and regulators' guidance, not instead of them.

From delivery to expertise

Litt California was founded in 2015 by Len May to offer naturally cultivated, California-grown cannabis to Los Angeles patients, delivered to their door. Years of growing, producing and delivering taught us what matters: consistent quality, careful processes and compliance you can prove. Today we share that know-how with growers and investors who want to do the same, wherever the law allows.
