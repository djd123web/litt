Training in Morocco

We teach strain selection and cultivation to operators working inside Morocco's regulated cannabis sector.

Is cannabis legal in Morocco?

Yes, within a licensed system. Law 13-21, adopted in 2021, permits cannabis for medical, pharmaceutical, cosmetic and industrial purposes. Recreational use remains illegal. A national regulator, the National Agency for the Regulation of Cannabis-related Activities (ANRAC), issues authorizations and oversees the sector.

Morocco's legal framework at a glance
TopicWhat public sources say
Permitted usesMedical, pharmaceutical, cosmetic and industrial. Recreational use is not allowed.
RegulatorANRAC, the national agency created under Law 13-21.
Where cultivation is allowedOnly in designated provinces of the Rif: Al Hoceima, Chefchaouen and Taounate.
Who may cultivateCultivation authorizations go to adult Moroccan nationals living in the authorized areas, organized in agricultural cooperatives.
Authorized activitiesNine licensed activities, including cultivation, nurseries, seed and plant import and export, processing, marketing and export.
Seeds and varietiesLocal variety (Beldia) and imported, certified seed. Higher-THC varieties are only allowed in the medical and pharmaceutical context.
Latest rulesA 2025 decree set conditions and obligations for cooperatives cultivating under the law.

News reports say ANRAC has issued several thousand authorizations and that the legal cultivation area has grown sharply since 2023. That growth is why growers and processors need dependable training on varieties and practice.

What we teach in Morocco

Our regular Morocco programs focus on strains, because choosing and managing the right varieties is one of the biggest levers on quality and income.

  • Strain selection

    Comparing the local Beldia and certified imported varieties for yield, cannabinoid and terpene profile, and suitability for each end use.

  • Growing in the Rif

    Adapting practice to altitude, heat, water and soil, from planting dates to irrigation and nutrition.

  • Harvest and post-harvest

    Harvest timing, drying, curing and storage that protect quality for pharmaceutical, cosmetic and industrial buyers.

  • Documentation and traceability

    Records and procedures that cooperatives need to satisfy the regulator and their buyers.

We also teach oil extraction and quality systems for licensed processors. See all training programs.

Staying inside the law

Our Morocco work is built around the licensed system. Training is for people and cooperatives operating, or preparing to operate, under Law 13-21. We only discuss genetics that are legally sourced through ANRAC-certified channels, we do not supply seeds, plants or products, and we encourage every client to confirm their authorization status with ANRAC and Moroccan counsel.

Foreign investors should note that cultivation authorizations are restricted to Moroccan nationals in the designated areas, so participation usually happens through other licensed activities or partnerships. Our licensing consulting can help you understand what is possible.
